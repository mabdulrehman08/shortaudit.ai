## Short Voiceover

I built this today: ShortAudit AI.

Most creators only find out a short is weak after they post it.

So this audits the short before upload.

You paste your idea or transcript, pick TikTok, Reels, or Shorts, and it scores the hook, retention risk, CTA, caption quality, platform fit, and whether it sounds too generic or AI-written.

I also added an Algorithm page so the score is not a fake black box. You can actually see what the system is checking.

Then I added a Fastlane-inspired homepage concept because the product should feel like a content machine, not a boring landing page.
